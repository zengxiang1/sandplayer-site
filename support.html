Getting started

  1. Open SandPlayer and choose your source type: Emby, Jellyfin, Plex, WebDAV or SMB.
  2. Enter your server address and sign in. Jellyfin also supports Quick Connect; Plex pairs with a code at plex.tv/link.
  3. Browse your libraries and press Play.

Frequently asked questions

Does SandPlayer include movies or shows?

SandPlayer does not include any movies or shows. You need your own media server or file share.

Why does the TV briefly go black when playback starts?

Apple TV is switching the frame rate / dynamic range to match the video. Enable Settings → Video and Audio → Match Content → Match Dynamic Range and Match Frame Rate on Apple TV for the best picture.

Why is playback slow to start or buffering?

The server may be busy or the connection too slow for the file’s bitrate. Try another route in Settings → Accounts & Routes, or check the server.

Why can’t I connect to a server on my home network?

Allow Local Network access for SandPlayer in Apple TV Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

How do I report a problem?

Email us with the title you were playing and the time it happened. You can also open Settings → Playback Diagnostics to view the local log.

快速开始

  1. 打开 SandPlayer，选择 Emby、Jellyfin、Plex、WebDAV 或 SMB 来源。
  2. 输入服务器地址并登录。Jellyfin 也支持快速连接；Plex 可在 plex.tv/link 输入配对码。
  3. 浏览媒体库并按下播放。

应用是否提供影片或剧集？

SandPlayer 不附带任何影片或剧集，你需要自备媒体服务器或文件共享。

为什么起播时电视短暂黑屏？

Apple TV 正在切换帧率或动态范围以匹配视频。建议在 Apple TV 的“设置 → 视频和音频 → 匹配内容”中开启“匹配动态范围”和“匹配帧率”。

为什么起播慢或一直缓冲？

服务器可能繁忙，或连接速度不足以承载文件码率。可在“设置 → 账号与线路”尝试其他线路，或检查服务器。

为什么无法连接家庭网络中的服务器？

在 Apple TV 的“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 本地网络”中允许 SandPlayer 访问本地网络。

如何报告问题？

请邮件提供播放的片名和问题发生的时间。也可在“设置 → 播放诊断”查看本地日志。
