Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · 简体中文

SandPlayer connects only to the media servers and file shares you configure.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. SandPlayer has no developer account system, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

  • Server addresses, preferences, watch history for file sources, and cached artwork are stored locally on your Apple TV.
  • Passwords and access tokens are stored in the Apple TV Keychain.
  • A small local diagnostics log helps troubleshoot playback. It redacts credentials and URL paths/query strings, stays on the device, and is never uploaded automatically.
  • The Top Shelf extension reads a local snapshot of your Continue Watching / Next Up items from the app’s shared container.

Communication with your servers

SandPlayer communicates directly with the servers you add (for example Emby, Jellyfin, Plex, WebDAV or SMB servers) to sign in, browse, stream and, where supported, report playback progress. When you pair with Plex, SandPlayer communicates with plex.tv to complete pairing and discover your servers. Those services are operated by you or third parties and have their own privacy practices.

Children

SandPlayer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日。SandPlayer 仅连接你配置的媒体服务器和文件共享。

我们收集的数据

我们不收集、存储、出售或分享任何个人数据。SandPlayer 没有开发者账号系统，不含广告、分析或跟踪。

存储在设备上的数据

  • 服务器地址、偏好设置、文件来源的观看记录和封面缓存存储在 Apple TV 本地。
  • 密码和访问令牌存储在 Apple TV 钥匙串中。
  • 本地的小型诊断日志用于排查播放问题，会隐藏凭据和 URL 路径、查询参数，保留在设备上，绝不自动上传。
  • Top Shelf 扩展从应用的共享容器读取“继续观看 / 下一集”的本地快照。

与你的服务器通信

SandPlayer 直接与你添加的服务器（如 Emby、Jellyfin、Plex、WebDAV 或 SMB）通信，以登录、浏览、播放，并在支持时报告播放进度。配对 Plex 时，应用会与 plex.tv 通信以完成配对并发现服务器。这些服务由你或第三方运营，适用各自的隐私规则。

儿童

SandPlayer 不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

政策变更

如政策变更，我们会更新此页面。
